The AI Architect

100+ Ways to Supercharge Your Creativity and Life

AI isn't a movie robot. It's a set of digital hands, and this book shows you what to give them.

This is the book that came before the seven pillars. It was written for anyone who keeps hearing about AI and wants to know what it's actually for, and it assumes no technical background at all. Its subject is how to use AI, not how to build it.

The first part explains what these tools are in ordinary terms. The long middle section is a directory: chapter by chapter, it goes through writing, images, video, audio, office work, marketing, education, business, and home life, and lists practical things you can hand over. The final chapters cover prompting, ethics, the changing job market, and how to choose the few skills worth learning first, since nobody needs all of them.

It has a wider audience than the pillar books and a wider scope. If the AI & Technology pillar is where you want to begin, start here.

What's inside

  1. Part I: The New FoundationThree chapters on what AI actually is, how it learned to see, hear, and speak, and what changes when tools begin to act for you.
  2. Part II: The Architect's DirectoryEleven chapters of practical uses by area: writing and publishing, images and design, video, audio and music, office work, marketing, strategy, gaming, education, business and coding, and home and health.
  3. Part III: Mastering the SystemFour chapters on automating life admin, writing better prompts, the ethics of using AI, and where the job market is heading.
  4. Part IV: The Final BlueprintA summary, a quick guide to which skills to learn first for your situation, and closing thoughts.

Is this book for you?

It's for you if

  • You're curious about AI and don't know where to begin.
  • You want everyday uses, not theory.
  • You'd like to see what's possible across work, home, and creative projects.

It isn't for you if

  • You're a developer looking for technical depth.
  • You want a detailed manual for one particular tool.

Questions readers ask

How does this fit with the seven pillars?
It doesn't belong to one. It's the book Jake wrote first, for a general audience, and it sits beside the series. The AI & Technology pillar will have its own foundation book.
Do I need technical knowledge?
No. The book says its job is to teach you how to use AI, not how to build one.
Is it only for men in their 50s?
No. Unlike the pillar books, this one is written for anyone, from students to retirees.
What are the 100+ ways?
Practical applications grouped by area of work and life. Most of them are in the directory chapters of Part II.
Will it tell me which tools to learn first?
Yes. The last part includes a short guide that matches a handful of skills to the kind of work you do, so you don't try to learn everything.
