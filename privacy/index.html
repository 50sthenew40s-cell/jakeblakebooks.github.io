Privacy

What this site collects

This site does not ask for your name or your email address. It has no accounts, no forms, and no newsletter, and it does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page. The site's typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser also makes a request to Google's servers.

Links to other sites

When you follow a link to a bookstore or a social media platform, you leave this site, and that company's own privacy policy applies.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if a newsletter is added, this page will be updated to say so. Last updated October 2026.
