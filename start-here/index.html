How the series is organized

Each pillar opens with a foundation book, which gives you the whole method for that area of your life: the framework, the tools, worked examples, the common mistakes, and a 90-day plan. After the foundation book come shorter books that go deep on one topic inside the pillar.

Every book stands on its own. You don't need to read them in order, and you don't need to read all of them. Two foundation books are available now, The Financial Architect and The Time Architect, and the others are being written.

If you can't decide

Start with time. Every other pillar asks for hours you probably don't have yet, and The Time Architect is about finding them.
