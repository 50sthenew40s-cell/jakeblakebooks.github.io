The Time Architect

How to Reclaim Your Life by Eliminating What Doesn't Matter

You have more experience, more credibility, and better judgment than you've ever had. What you don't have is time. This book is a method for getting your hours back by removing what doesn't belong in your week, with a seven-day audit, four decisions, free tools, and a 90-day plan.

Who is Jake Blake?

I'm Jake Blake. I spent twenty-five years climbing the corporate ladder, and at 48 I was working more than fifty hours a week and wondering where the weeks were going.

I stopped playing by the old rules and rebuilt my money, my time, and my work one piece at a time. I'm 54 now, my time is my own, and these books are my field notes on how I did it.
