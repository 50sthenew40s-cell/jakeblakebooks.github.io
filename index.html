About Jake Blake

Jake Blake spent 25 years climbing the corporate ladder. At 50, he realized he had security but no time—and time was the only thing he couldn't get back. So he got strategic and redesigned his entire life on his own terms.

He writes about all of it—field notes from someone actually living it.
