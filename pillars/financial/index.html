Financial

Building wealth when time is short.

Nothing on this page is financial advice. Talk to a qualified financial professional before you make investment decisions.

Most money advice assumes you started at 25 and have forty years to let it compound. If you're 50, you don't, and that changes both the math and the method. This pillar is about building financial independence on a shorter runway: knowing your own number, adding income beyond a single salary, and automating the parts that would otherwise depend on willpower.

It's for the man who earns a decent living and still feels behind, who started late or got knocked back along the way, and who wants a realistic plan instead of a lottery ticket. You won't find crypto, options trading, or anything that promises to make you rich quickly.

The books in this pillar use plain arithmetic and honest timelines. The figures in them are examples to learn from, and they say so.

I started at 48. Almost everything I read assumed I had started at 25.
