Relationships

Building the connections that matter after 50.

Relationships change after 50. Marriages carry long histories, children become adults, parents start to need you, and friendships thin out unless you tend them. They change again when you start redesigning your own life, because the people around you have opinions about it. This pillar is about being deliberate with the people who matter.

It's for the man in a long marriage, the man starting over, the father of grown children, and the son of aging parents, who are often the same man.

The books will talk plainly and respect how complicated real families are. They won't offer formulas.

At 50 you know yourself. Use that to build relationships that actually work.
