AI & Technology

Putting the tools to work without the hype.

AI is a tool, and a man of 50 is better placed to use it than most people assume, because he has the judgment to tell a good draft from a bad one. This pillar is about the small set of tools that actually save time and money, how to use them without becoming a technician, and how to ignore the rest.

It's for the man who feels the technology is moving past him, who has no interest in theory, and who needs to see for himself that he can learn this.

The books will put free tools first and keep things simple. Until this pillar's foundation book is ready, The AI Architect, the first book I wrote, is the place to begin.

I use a handful of AI tools every day. None of them is complicated.
