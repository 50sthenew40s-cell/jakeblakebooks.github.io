Health

Staying sharp and energetic for the decades ahead.

Nothing on this page is medical advice. Talk to your doctor before you change your exercise, diet, sleep, or medication.

This pillar isn't about fitness magazines or looking 30. It's about maintenance: sleep, strength, energy, and a clear head, so that the next thirty years are ones you can actually use. Health is the asset nobody values until it starts to slip, and the 50s are when the decisions begin to count.

It's for the man whose energy isn't what it was, who recovers more slowly than he did at 40, and who wants to stay capable without turning his life into a training program.

The books will be plain about what's worth doing and what's noise. They're one man's notes on what he learned, and they'll tell you to check with your doctor before you change anything that matters.

I'm not trying to look young. I'm trying to still be in the game at 80.
