Time

Getting your hours back by cutting what doesn't matter.

At 50 you have more experience, more credibility, and better judgment than you've ever had, and less time. Productivity advice doesn't help, because it's written for people who want to fit more in. This pillar is about fitting less in: finding out where your hours really go, deciding what shouldn't be on your plate at all, and protecting what you win back.

It's for the man whose week is full of meetings he didn't call, email that refills faster than he can clear it, and obligations he agreed to once and inherited forever, whether those are at work or at home.

The books here are practical guides, with audits, word-for-word scripts, free tools, and AI prompts. They won't teach you to get more done in a day.

I got back more than twenty hours a week, and I didn't do it by working faster. I did it by cutting the wrong work.
