Career

Using your experience as leverage, whether you stay or go.

Twenty-five years of experience is either your biggest advantage or the thing that gets you quietly sidelined, depending on how you use it. This pillar covers both paths: staying where you are and making the job work for you, or building toward a move on your own terms. It deals honestly with ageism, and with what AI is changing at work.

It's for the man who is passed over for the interesting assignments, who wonders whether he's still relevant, or who simply knows he doesn't want another fifteen years of the same.

The books will be about strategy, not desperation. They won't pretend that age discrimination doesn't exist, and they won't tell you to act 35.

Your twenty-five years are your unfair advantage, if you position them right.
